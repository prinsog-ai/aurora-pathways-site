How It Works

Create a tier. Set your monthly price in USDC. Define what subscribers get — exclusive content, community access, early releases.

Subscribers join. They pay once. The contract verifies the payment, timestamps the subscription, and grants access. No middleman. No payment processor. No platform that can change the terms.

Content unlocks. Verified subscription = automatic content access. When a subscription expires, access revokes. The contract enforces the rules — not a platform's content moderation team.

Get paid instantly. Funds flow directly to the creator's wallet at the moment of subscription. No monthly payout schedule. No minimum threshold. USDC in your wallet in seconds.
