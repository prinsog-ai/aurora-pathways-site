FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Straight answers. No whitepaper-speak.

Do I need to understand crypto to work with you?

No. We handle all the technical parts. You tell us what your business needs, we build it. We'll explain what matters for your workflow — you don't need to know what a Merkle tree is.

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects ship in 2–4 weeks. Basic decentralized websites can go live in under a week. Full dApp ecosystems with smart contracts take longer — we'll give you a clear timeline after the discovery call.

Is my data actually safe on a blockchain?

Different from "safe in a database." Your data is replicated across thousands of nodes — no single point of failure. No company can lose it, delete it, or sell it. For sensitive data, we use encryption and IPFS with access controls. You hold the keys.

What if I change my mind about Web3 later?

Your site also lives on a regular .xyz domain — customers can access it through any normal browser. The Web3 layer adds capabilities, it doesn't replace the basics. You're not locked into anything.

Why Polygon and Base instead of Ethereum?

Ethereum mainnet costs $5–50+ per transaction. Polygon and Base cost under a penny. Same security model, same tools, fraction of the price. For business use, it's not even close.
