## 2. PROJECT TIMELINE

---

**Typical Web 3.0 Website Build — 4 Weeks**

Below is the standard roadmap for a Web 3.0 website project. Each week has clear deliverables (what we produce) and responsibilities (what we need from you). Milestones marked with ★ are approval gates: we don't move forward until you're satisfied.

---

### Week 1: Discovery ★

| Focus | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Kickoff Call** | 60-minute deep dive: your business goals, audience, brand identity, and vision for Web 3.0. |
| **Technical Audit** | We review your current digital presence, hosting, domains, and any existing blockchain integrations. |
| **Requirements Doc** | A plain-English document capturing every feature, page, and integration for the site. |
| **Sitemap & Wireframes** | Low-fidelity roadmap of your site structure and page layouts. |

**Milestone Deliverable:** Requirements Document + Sitemap approved by you.  
**Client Responsibility:** Attend kickoff call; provide brand assets (logos, color palette, fonts); share 2-3 competitor/reference sites you admire.  

> **★ Approval Gate:** We pause here until you sign off on scope. This protects your budget and our timeline.

---

### Week 2: Design ★

| Focus | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Moodboards** | 2-3 visual directions reflecting your brand personality and Web 3.0 aesthetic. |
| **High-Fidelity Mockups** | Pixel-perfect designs for homepage + 2 key interior pages, in both desktop and mobile views. |
| **Design System** | Typography scale, color tokens, component library — the building blocks of a consistent site. |
| **Revision Round** | One round of revisions based on your feedback, included in the package. |

**Milestone Deliverable:** Finalized high-fidelity mockups, approved by you.  
**Client Responsibility:** Review moodboards within 2 business days; provide consolidated feedback (one point person); approve final mockups.

> **★ Approval Gate:** We don't write a line of code until the design is locked. Changes after this point may affect timeline or scope.

---

### Week 3: Development

| Focus | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Frontend Build** | All pages built, responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile. |
| **Web 3.0 Integration** | Wallet connectivity, smart contract interactions, NFT/token-gated content (as scoped), decentralized identity (DID) integration. |
| **CMS Setup** | Content management system configured so you can update copy, images, and blog posts without touching code. |
| **Content Population** | We load in your approved copy and imagery; placeholder content replaced with real assets. |
| **Internal QA** | Cross-browser testing, performance audits, accessibility checks, security review. |

**Milestone Deliverable:** Staging site live at a private URL for your review.  
**Client Responsibility:** Provide final copy and images by end of Week 2; review staging site within 3 business days; submit a single consolidated list of changes.

---

### Week 4: Launch

| Focus | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Final Revisions** | Address your staging feedback. Minor adjustments only; major scope changes go to a Phase 2 project. |
| **Pre-Launch Checklist** | SSL certs, DNS configuration, wallet connection tests, smart contract verification (if applicable), backup and recovery plan. |
| **Production Deployment** | Site pushed live to your domain. Post-launch smoke testing: every link, every form, every Web 3.0 feature verified. |
| **Handoff & Training** | 60-minute walkthrough of your CMS, wallet management, and basic site administration. |
| **30-Day Support Window** | Bug fixes and minor adjustments at no charge for 30 days post-launch. |

**Milestone Deliverable:** 🚀 **Your Web 3.0 website is LIVE.**  
**Client Responsibility:** Point your domain to our hosting (we'll walk you through it); attend handoff training; celebrate.

---

- **Content updates** (up to agreed hours/month)

## 4. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

---

### 1. "What actually IS a Web 3.0 website? How is it different from what I have now?"

A traditional (Web 2.0) website lives on a single company's servers — think GoDaddy, AWS, or Squarespace. A Web 3.0 website adds a layer of decentralization: it can connect to blockchains, let your customers log in with a crypto wallet instead of a username/password, and host token-gated content that only certain holders can access. It's not about replacing your current site — it's about adding capabilities that weren't possible before. Think of it as upgrading from a storefront to a storefront with a membership club, loyalty program, and direct customer-to-customer marketplace built in.

---

### 2. "Do I need to understand blockchain or crypto to use this site?"

No. That's our job. You'll be able to update your content through a simple dashboard, the same way you'd edit a WordPress site. The Web 3.0 features work behind the scenes. Your customers who ARE crypto-native will appreciate the wallet login and token-gated perks; your customers who aren't can still browse and interact the traditional way. We build for both audiences.

---

### 3. "Will my customers need a crypto wallet to use my site?"

Not unless you want them to. Most of our builds use a "progressive" approach: the core site is accessible to everyone, and the Web 3.0 features (wallet login, token-gated content, NFT perks) are optional layers for the customers who have wallets. You don't shut anyone out — you give your Web 3.0-savvy audience extra reasons to engage.

---

### 4. "Is this secure? I hear about hacks and scams in crypto all the time."

We take security extremely seriously — it's not an afterthought. Every site we build includes SSL encryption, hardened wallet connections, audited smart contract patterns (where applicable), and a security review before launch. The blockchain horror stories you hear are almost always about poorly written smart contracts or phishing scams targeting individuals, not about professionally built websites. We follow industry best practices and test everything thoroughly. Your site will be as secure — and in many ways more secure — than a traditional website.

---

### 5. "How much of my time is actually needed? I'm running a business."

We've designed our process to be lean on your time. Here's the realistic commitment:

- **Week 1:** One 60-minute kickoff call
- **Weeks 1-4:** One 30-minute check-in call per week
- **Between calls:** Reviewing mockups and providing feedback (usually 1-2 hours total across the month, spread out)

Total: about 4-6 hours of your attention over four weeks. We handle everything else.

---

### 6. "What if I want changes after the site is live?"

Small tweaks and bug fixes are free for the first 30 days after launch — we expect a few adjustments as you settle in. After that, changes are handled through our monthly management retainer (starting at $500/month, which includes content update hours), or as a separate Phase 2 project if you want major new features. No surprise invoices, no "gotcha" fees. We'll always quote before we work.

---

### 7. "Can my existing domain and hosting stay the same? I don't want to lose my search rankings."

Yes. We'll connect your new Web 3.0 site to your existing domain — you keep your URL, your SEO history, and your email. The transition is handled carefully to minimize any disruption. Your domain stays registered with your current provider; we just point it to the new hosting environment. If you're nervous about SEO, our Advanced and Premium tiers include an SEO foundation package to protect and improve your rankings through the transition.

---

### 8. "What happens if the blockchain technology changes? Will my site break?"

Blockchains evolve, and we build with that in mind. Our integrations use standard, widely adopted protocols (like WalletConnect for wallet connections and widely-used smart contract standards). We don't bet on experimental or single-chain solutions. Additionally, our monthly management retainers include monitoring and updates — if a protocol upgrade requires a site change, we handle it proactively. You won't wake up to a broken site.

---

### 9. "Why should I do this now instead of waiting?"

Two reasons. First, early adopters capture market attention. Businesses that move into Web 3.0 now — while it's still novel — get press, word-of-mouth, and a reputation as forward-thinkers in their industry. Second, the infrastructure is ready. Wallet adoption is high enough that millions of potential customers can already use your Web 3.0 features. Waiting another year or two means competing in a much more crowded space. The window to stand out is open now.

---

### 10. "What if I'm not happy with the final product?"

We don't consider the site done until you're satisfied. Our process builds in approval gates at the end of Discovery (Week 1) and Design (Week 2) specifically to make sure we're aligned before we build. If you're unhappy during development, we pause and address it. Our goal is a long-term relationship — and that only works if you love what we've built. In practice, because we stay in close communication, this rarely becomes an issue. But if it does, we work it out. Your 50% final payment is only due when you approve the launch.
