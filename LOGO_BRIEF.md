# Aurora Pathways — Logo Design Brief

## Company
**Name:** Aurora Pathways
**Industry:** Web3 consulting — blockchain solutions for real estate, energy, and enterprise
**Website:** aurorapathways.xyz

## Brand Essence
- **Aurora** = dawn, light, new beginnings, insight
- **Pathways** = direction, guided journey, clarity, progress
- **Together** = illuminating the path forward for businesses

## Logo Concept

### The Symbol
A stylized aurora borealis ribbon or arc in the sky, combined with a subtle pathway leading toward it. The aurora represents insight and innovation. The pathway represents the guided journey we provide to clients.

### Required Elements
1. **Aurora ribbon/arc** — flowing, wave-like shape in the sky using smooth gradients
2. **Pathway/road** — a subtle winding line leading toward the aurora, suggesting direction, transformation, and progress
3. **Optional: minimal horizon/hills** — kept very minimal so aurora + pathway remain the focus

### Style
- Vector, flat or semi-flat design (no photo realism)
- Clean, modern, minimalist
- Smooth gradients (not harsh transitions)
- Calm, inspiring, forward-looking
- NOT childish or whimsical
- Must scale well: website favicon, social avatar, business cards, large format

## Color Palette

### Background
- Deep midnight navy: `#0a0e1a` or `#0f1629`
- Charcoal alternative: `#1a1a2e`

### Aurora Gradients (smooth transitions between these)
- Teal: `#4ec9b0`
- Emerald: `#2dd4a0`
- Violet: `#7c5cfc`
- Soft magenta: `#c084fc`
- Lavender: `#a78bfa`

### Pathway
- Pale teal: `#b0e8d8` or off-white: `#e0f0eb`

### Text
- White or very light gray on dark backgrounds

## Typography
- Clean sans-serif, slightly geometric and contemporary
- Suggestions: Inter, DM Sans, Outfit, General Sans, Satoshi
- "Aurora" slightly emphasized (bolder weight or larger)
- "Pathways" in lighter weight
- Strong letter-spacing, balanced alignment

## Logo Variations Needed

### 1. Full Lockup (icon + wordmark)
- Icon on left, "Aurora Pathways" text on right (horizontal)
- OR icon centered above, text centered below (vertical)
- Primary use: website header, proposals, presentations

### 2. Icon Only
- Just the aurora + pathway symbol
- Use: favicon, social media avatar, app icon, watermark

### 3. Wordmark Only
- Just "Aurora Pathways" text
- Use: email signature, documents, where icon doesn't fit

### 4. Dark Background Version
- Full color on dark navy/charcoal background
- Primary version

### 5. Light Background Version
- Adjusted colors for white/light backgrounds
- Aurora colors may need to be slightly deeper/more saturated

## What NOT To Do
- ❌ No realistic aurora photography
- ❌ No cartoonish or childish illustrations
- ❌ No overly complex or intricate details (must be simple at small sizes)
- ❌ No generic "globe" or "network node" clichés
- ❌ No literal lightbulbs or arrows
- ❌ No clip art aesthetic

## Reference Brands (for aesthetic feel)
- **Linear** — clean, minimal, geometric
- **Stripe** — professional, precise, modern
- **Vercel** — stark, confident, simple
- **Notion** — friendly but professional
- **Figma** — colorful but clean
