## THE MODEL

This isn't a traditional agency. It's an **AI-orchestrated service business** — one human (you) directing a team of specialized AI sub-agents that handle outreach, content, development, and operations. Think of it as Harvey from Suits, cloned into a mini-army, each with a specific role.

Your job: strategy, closing, and quality control. Their job: everything else.
