From opaque loan lifecycles and T+2 settlement to transparent, instant, auditable lendingDe ciclos de préstamo opacos y liquidación T+2 a préstamos transparentes, instantáneos y auditables

✕ Before Aurora

  • Loan lifecycle tracking spans multiple systems — origination, servicing, secondary market
  • Settlement takes T+2, tying up capital and creating counterparty risk
  • Compliance reports require manual compilation from siloed data
  • Investors can't independently verify loan provenance
  • Securitization opacity creates regulatory and reputational risk

✓ After Aurora

  • Complete loan provenance from origination to secondary market — one chain
  • Instant P2P settlement — no T+2 delay, no counterparty risk window
  • Regulatory reports generated in seconds from on-chain data
  • Investors independently verify any loan's history and status
  • Transparent securitization builds trust with regulators and investors
