Get USDC on Polygon

You need USDC on Polygon to book rides. Here's how to get it.

1 Transfer from an Exchange

Withdraw USDC from Coinbase, Binance, or Kraken directly to your wallet on the Polygon network.

USDC contract: 0x3c499c542cEF5E3811e1192ce70d8cC03d5c3359

2 Bridge from Ethereum

Use the Polygon Portal Bridge to bridge USDC from Ethereum to Polygon.

3 Swap MATIC for USDC

If you have MATIC, swap it for USDC on Uniswap or QuickSwap.

New to Web3? Get started in 3 steps

RideP2P runs on blockchain — but don't worry, it's simpler than it sounds.

1

Install a wallet

A wallet is like a digital bank account. MetaMask is the most popular — it's a browser extension that takes 30 seconds to install.

Download MetaMask →
2

Add funds to your wallet

You'll need a small amount of USDC (a digital dollar) to pay for rides. You can buy it directly in MetaMask or transfer from an exchange like Coinbase.

3

Connect and start

Come back here, click "Connect Wallet," and you're in. Find rides, post rides, and pay — all secured by escrow.
