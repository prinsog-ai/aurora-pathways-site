Get USDC on Polygon

You need USDC on Polygon to participate in prediction markets

1

Bridge from another chain

Use Jumper Exchange, Stargate, or Orbiter Finance to bridge USDC from Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, or other chains to Polygon.

2

Buy on a CEX and withdraw

Purchase USDC on exchanges like Coinbase, Kraken, or Binance, then withdraw directly to your Polygon wallet. Select Polygon (MATIC) as the network when withdrawing.

3

Swap on a DEX

If you have MATIC or other tokens on Polygon, swap them for USDC on Uniswap, QuickSwap, or SushiSwap.

4

Verify your USDC

The USDC contract on Polygon is 0x3c499c...c3359. Always verify the contract address before transacting.

New to Web3? Get started in 3 steps

Forekast runs on blockchain — but don't worry, it's simpler than it sounds.

1

Install a wallet

A wallet is like a digital bank account. MetaMask is the most popular — it's a browser extension that takes 30 seconds to install.

Download MetaMask →
2

Add funds to your wallet

You'll need a small amount of USDC (a digital dollar) to trade on prediction markets. You can buy it directly in MetaMask or transfer from an exchange like Coinbase.

3

Connect and start

Come back here, click "Connect Wallet," and you're in. Browse markets, make predictions, and earn when you're right.
