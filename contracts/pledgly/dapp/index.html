New to Web3? Get started in 3 steps

Pledgly runs on blockchain — but don't worry, it's simpler than it sounds.

1

Install a wallet

A wallet is like a digital bank account. MetaMask is the most popular — it's a browser extension that takes 30 seconds to install.

Download MetaMask →
2

Add funds to your wallet

You'll need a small amount of USDC (a digital dollar) to subscribe to creators. You can buy it directly in MetaMask or transfer from an exchange like Coinbase.

3

Connect and start

Come back here, click "Connect Wallet," and you're in. Browse creators, subscribe, and support the people you love — directly.
