Questions
Frequently asked.
How do I book a trailer?

Use the booking form above, call us, text us, or email. We confirm availability within 24 hours and hold your trailer once confirmed. No payment required until pickup.

What's the deposit?

$50 per trailer. Collected at pickup (cash, check, or card). Returned same day on safe return. We document trailer condition with photos at both pickup and return — no disputes.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver anywhere in the Williston Basin — Williston, Tioga, Watford City, Stanley, and surrounding areas. Delivery fee depends on distance. Call for a quote.

What if I damage the trailer?

We take photos at pickup and return. If there's new damage, we get a repair estimate and deduct it from your deposit. If the repair costs more than the deposit, we'll work with you on a fair resolution. Normal wear and tear is expected.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes — just call or text us before your return date. We'll extend at the same rate if the trailer isn't booked. If it is, we'll work with you on the earliest return time.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup. Within 48 hours, we charge one day rate. No-shows are charged the full first day. We're reasonable — just communicate with us.

Do I need my own insurance?

We carry liability insurance on our trailers. However, we recommend you have auto/truck insurance that covers towed equipment. Check with your provider — most commercial auto policies cover rental trailers.

What hitch do I need?

The dump trailer uses a 2-5/16" adjustable height Demco coupler. The flatbed uses a standard 2-5/16" ball. Both trailers have electric brakes — you'll need a brake controller in your truck.

Our Process
No surprises. Ever.
We document everything so you never have to worry about damage disputes.

📸 At Pickup

Before you leave our yard, we walk around the trailer together and photograph:

  • All four sides + roof/top
  • Tire condition and tread
  • Lights, brakes, wiring
  • Deck/floor condition
  • Coupler and jack
  • Any existing damage noted

📸 At Return

When you bring it back, we do the same walk-around:

  • Same photo angles as pickup
  • Compare against pickup photos
  • Any new damage discussed on the spot
  • Fair repair estimate if needed
  • Deposit returned same day if no issues
  • Photos shared with you for your records
