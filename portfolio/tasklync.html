Tasklync — Decentralized Freelance Marketplace

Permissionless global work. 3% fee vs Upwork's 10–20%. Instant USDC settlement. No arbitrary bans.

💔 The Problem

Upwork takes 10–20% of every contract. Fiverr takes 20% flat. Freelancers wait 5+ days for ACH settlement while platforms earn float on your money. Accounts get banned by opaque algorithms with no appeal. A freelancer in Lagos pays the same 20% as one in London — extortionate for developing-world talent.

Tasklync replaces the platform with smart contracts. No rent-seeking middleman. No geographic discrimination. Code enforces the deal, not a trust & safety team in San Francisco.

🔄 How It Works

1
Client posts job with milestones, deadline, and budget in USDC
2
USDC locked in escrow — TaskEscrow.sol holds funds, visible on-chain
3
Freelancer delivers — submits work via IPFS with cryptographic timestamp
4
Client approves → milestone auto-releases to freelancer's wallet in seconds
5
Disputes? Staked validators vote. Majority rules. No customer support ticket queue.

Total protocol fee: 3%. That's it. No graduated fee tiers, no "plus" plans, no hidden percentage bumps.

🔑 Key Features

📦 Milestone Payments
Break projects into sequential milestones. Each milestone releases independently. No "100% upfront" risk.

📎 IPFS Deliverables
All work submissions stored on IPFS with cryptographic hashes. Tamper-proof proof-of-delivery with timestamps.

⭐ On-Chain Reputation
Immutable job history, ratings, and skill tags. Portable across platforms. No platform can delete your track record.

⚖️ Staked Validator Disputes
Token-staked validators adjudicate disputes. Slashing penalties for dishonest rulings. No corporate override.
