Forekast — Truly Decentralized Prediction Markets

Permissionless markets. Community resolution. No corporate override. 1% market fee shared with token stakers.

🎲 The Problem

Polymarket proved prediction markets work — $6.8B+ in volume during the 2024 US election alone. But they also proved the centralization problem: geo-restrictions locked out users globally, centralized resolution let a single entity decide market outcomes, and markets can be censored by corporate policy at any moment. A prediction market controlled by a Delaware LLC is not truly permissionless.

Forekast builds the same product with zero centralized control. Multi-oracle resolution, staked validators, and fully permissionless market creation. No CEO can pull your market. No compliance department can block your country.

🔄 How It Works

1
Create a market — anyone can create a YES/NO market. Define the question, resolution source, and expiry date. Stake USDC as creator.
2
Trade shares — buy YES or NO outcome shares at market-implied probabilities. AMM-based pricing with deep liquidity pools.
3
Oracle resolves — at expiry, registered oracles submit resolution. Multi-oracle consensus required (≥3 of 5).
4
Winners claim — winning shares redeemable for USDC at $1.00/share. Instant settlement on Base L2. No withdrawal queue.
5
Disputes? — challenge period open for 24h after resolution. Staked validators vote. Final and irreversible.

🔑 Key Features

🔮 Multi-Oracle Resolution
Markets resolved by consensus of ≥3 of 5 registered oracles. No single point of failure. Oracles stake tokens as bond.

⚖️ Staked Validators
Dispute resolution by validators who stake $PREDICT. Honest validators earn fees. Dishonest ones get slashed.

🌍 Permissionless Markets
Anyone anywhere can create a market. No approval process. No jurisdiction blocking. The market exists as long as the chain does.

⚡ Instant Settlement
Winning shares redeem for USDC immediately upon resolution. No withdrawal queue. No "processing time." Base L2 confirms in ~2 seconds.
