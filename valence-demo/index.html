How the Carbon Credit Platform Works

From flare capture to revenue — fully automated on Polygon blockchain.

📝
1. Record
Valence logs flare capture volumes from each site
✅
2. Verify
Data is signed on-chain. Customer can dispute within 48hrs
🪙
3. Tokenize
Smart contract mints 1 ERC-20 token per ton CO2 reduced
💰
4. Distribute
Credits auto-split to Valence + site owner wallets

🔗 Ownership Problem — Solved

The smart contract eliminates the "who owns the credits" question. Both parties agree on the split percentage when the site is onboarded. After that, every credit is distributed automatically.

Valence 60%
Site Owner 40%

Configurable per site. Can be 50/50, 60/40, 70/30 — whatever you negotiate.

⛓️ Why Blockchain?

  • ✅ Immutable — no one can alter past records
  • ✅ Transparent — both parties see all data in real time
  • ✅ Automatic — no spreadsheets, no reconciliation
  • ✅ Auditable — ESG reports generate from on-chain data
  • ✅ Tradeable — credits can be sold on carbon marketplaces
