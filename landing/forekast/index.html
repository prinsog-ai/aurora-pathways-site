The Problem

Prediction markets are
only as fair as the referee

Polymarket can freeze markets. Kalshi can pause trading. Every centralized prediction platform has a kill switch — and someone holding it. When the outcome is contested, whoever controls the platform decides what's "true."

🎲

Centralized = controlled

One company decides which markets exist, when they resolve, and what counts as the correct outcome. That's not a prediction market. That's a casino with a house edge.

🔮

Forekast removes the referee

Markets resolve through multiple independent oracles. If they disagree, token holders vote. No single entity can rig the outcome. The market is the authority.

How It Works

Create. Trade. Resolve.
No referee needed.

1

Someone creates a market

Propose a question: "Will Bitcoin hit $200K by December 2026?" Set the resolution date. Choose oracles. Fund initial liquidity.

2

Traders buy YES or NO tokens

Prices reflect collective belief. YES at $0.65 means the market thinks there's a 65% chance. Trade anytime before resolution.

3

Resolution date arrives

Oracles report the outcome. Multiple sources confirm. Consensus is validated on-chain.

4

Winners get paid. Losers don't.

Correct token holders redeem for $1.00 each. Wrong token holders get nothing. Smart contract handles everything.
