How It Works

Four steps. No middleman.

1

Client posts a job with USDC in escrow

The contract holds the funds. They're visible on-chain. Nobody can touch them until the work is done.

2

Freelancers stake to apply

A small USDC deposit filters out bots and spam. If you're accepted, you get it back. If you flake, it goes to the client.

3

Work gets done, milestones get approved

Client reviews and approves. Funds move from escrow to freelancer. Seconds, not days.

4

Both sides rate each other on-chain

Reputation that can't be gamed or deleted. Your track record belongs to you.
