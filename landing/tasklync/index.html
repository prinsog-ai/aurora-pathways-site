Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

How do I get paid?

When a client approves your work, USDC is released from escrow directly to your wallet — instantly. No waiting for bank transfers or payment processors.

What if there's a dispute?

If a client and freelancer can't agree, either party can open a dispute. The case goes to community vote, and the funds are released based on the outcome.

Do I need crypto experience?

You'll need a wallet (like MetaMask) and some MATIC for gas on Polygon. If you've never used crypto before, it takes about 10 minutes to set up.

What chains do you support?

Tasklync is currently deployed on Polygon mainnet — chosen for its low fees and fast transactions. More chains may be added in the future.
