FAQ

Common questions

How do fans subscribe? ▼

Fans connect a crypto wallet (like MetaMask or Phantom) to the Pledgly dApp, choose a membership tier, and pay. An NFT is minted to their wallet that grants access to the creator's exclusive content.

Can I use regular money? ▼

Yes. Pledgly integrates on-ramp providers so fans can pay with a credit card or bank transfer. The payment is converted to USDC behind the scenes so the creator still receives instant crypto payouts.

What if I'm not crypto-savvy? ▼

Pledgly is designed to feel like a normal web app. You don't need to understand blockchain to use it — the dApp handles wallets, NFTs, and payouts in the background. If you can use Patreon, you can use Pledgly.

Can I migrate from Patreon? ▼

Yes. You can import your tier structure and invite your existing subscribers to claim their membership NFT on Pledgly. We're building migration tools to make this as smooth as possible.
