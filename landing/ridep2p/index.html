The Problem

Uber takes 40%. Drivers
should take 100%.

Uber's take rate averages 30-40%. On a $25 ride, the driver might see $15. The corporation collects billions while drivers set their own hours, maintain their own cars, and bear all the risk. The platform provides a map and a payment button — that's not worth 40%.

😤

The corporation owns the network

Uber controls pricing, surge multipliers, driver ratings, and who gets banned. Drivers are treated as contractors with zero ownership in the platform they built.

🔑

Ridep2p gives the network to the drivers

Governance tokens go to drivers and riders. They vote on fees, rules, and upgrades. The protocol collects nothing. Every cent goes to the driver.

How It Works

Request. Ride. Release.
That's it.

1

Rider requests a trip

Enter pickup and drop-off. Smart contract calculates the fare. Rider deposits USDC into escrow. Funds are locked and visible.

2

Driver accepts and picks up

Nearby drivers see the request. First to accept gets the fare locked. Rider confirms pickup in the app.

3

Driver completes the ride

Arrival confirmed. Smart contract releases USDC from escrow to the driver's wallet. Instant. Irreversible.

4

Both rate each other on-chain

Ratings are permanent and portable. Good drivers build trust. Bad actors get filtered by the community.
